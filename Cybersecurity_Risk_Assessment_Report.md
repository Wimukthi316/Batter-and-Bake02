## Introduction (3 marks)
### Background on e-commerce cybersecurity challenges
E-commerce platforms face a range of cybersecurity challenges, including data breaches, payment fraud, and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks. These challenges are exacerbated by the growing sophistication of cyber threats and the increasing reliance on digital transactions.

### Project objectives and significance
The primary objective of this project is to assess the cybersecurity risks associated with e-commerce platforms and develop mitigation strategies to enhance security posture. This assessment is significant for ensuring the protection of sensitive data and maintaining customer trust.

### Report structure overview
This report is structured to encompass the critical aspects of cybersecurity risk assessment, including project planning, risk management strategies, and team collaboration frameworks.

### Scope of the assessment
The scope of this assessment includes identifying potential cybersecurity threats, evaluating vulnerabilities, and recommending appropriate security controls for e-commerce platforms.
